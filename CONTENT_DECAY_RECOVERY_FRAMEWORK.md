# Content Decay Recovery Framework

Date: 2026-08-10
Site: https://www.editandproofread.com/

## Review Cadence

- Review Search Console performance monthly for priority commercial pages and quarterly for supporting blog pages.
- Compare clicks, impressions, average position, CTR, indexed status, and primary queries against the prior 28 days and prior 3 months.
- Prioritize pages with falling impressions, falling clicks, low CTR at visible positions, or "Crawled - currently not indexed" / "Discovered - currently not indexed" status.

## Triage Rules

- Keep pages where the intent is distinct and useful.
- Improve pages where the topic is still relevant but content, internal links, schema, examples, or title/meta need refresh.
- Consolidate only when two pages clearly satisfy the same search intent and one page cannot be differentiated.
- Noindex only thin utility/private pages, not ranking pages with a recoverable search purpose.
- Delete only when a page is obsolete, has no strategic value, has no useful backlinks, and cannot be improved truthfully.

## Refresh Checklist

- Confirm the target query and search intent.
- Update title and meta description only when CTR or intent alignment is weak.
- Add missing expert workflow detail, examples, limitations, FAQs, or decision criteria.
- Add contextual links from relevant articles and service pages.
- Add links out to related service, pricing, submission, or policy pages where useful.
- Update `dateUpdated` only when meaningful content changed.
- Check canonical, indexability, structured data, sitemap lastmod, and final HTTP status.
- Rebuild and run SEO tests before deployment.

## Measurement

- Record the pre-update URL, date, target query, issue, content change, internal links added, and lastmod value.
- Inspect the URL in Google Search Console after deployment.
- Recheck indexing and performance after 2, 4, and 8 weeks.
- Do not repeatedly rewrite a page before Google has had time to recrawl and evaluate it.

## Guardrails

- Do not create fake author credentials, editor profiles, study results, awards, ratings, reviews, or testimonials.
- Do not stamp all sitemap URLs with today's date.
- Do not merge informational and commercial pages when users need both intents.
- Do not build programmatic SEO pages without unique expert value and manual review.
- Do not buy links or use spam backlink packages.
