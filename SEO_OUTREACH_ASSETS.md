## Concise Company Bio

My Editing and Proofreading Desk is a professional human editing and proofreading service for students, researchers, authors, businesses, and professionals. The desk supports final proofreading, deeper editing, academic documents, manuscripts, business files, CVs, translated English, and formatting-ready submissions while preserving the author's meaning and responsibility for final approval.

## Service Descriptions

- Professional proofreading: final grammar, punctuation, spelling, consistency, and presentation review for complete drafts.
- Professional editing: clarity, flow, tone, structure, sentence quality, and readability improvement for drafts that need deeper work.
- Thesis editing: human editing for master's and PhD thesis drafts, focused on academic tone, sentence clarity, chapter flow, terminology, and consistency.
- Dissertation proofreading: whole-document final review for dissertations, including language, references, formatting, tables, figures, and final-file readiness.
- Document formatting: formatting support for Word styles, headings, section breaks, tables, figures, captions, references, page numbering, contents lists, and final PDFs.
