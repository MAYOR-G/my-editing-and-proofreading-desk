## Decisions

- No nofollow attributes were added to ordinary internal links.
- No artificial sitewide link blocks were added.
- Priority commercial pages receive contextual support from related informational pages.
- Internal anchors intentionally vary so the site does not repeat the same exact-match phrase everywhere.
