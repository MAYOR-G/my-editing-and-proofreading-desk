# My Editing and Proofreading Desk Knowledge Base

This document is intended for the AI support and retrieval system for My Editing and Proofreading Desk. It should be used as grounded support knowledge for answering customer questions about services, submissions, pricing, payments, security, dashboard usage, and support.

## Company Overview

My Editing and Proofreading Desk is a professional editing and proofreading website for people who need clear, polished, trustworthy documents.

The website serves students, researchers, authors, professionals, businesses, applicants, and general clients who need editorial support for academic, business, technical, professional, and personal documents.

The main value proposition is human-led editorial review. Editors improve grammar, clarity, structure, flow, tone, formatting, and reader confidence while preserving the writer's original meaning and voice.

The website also includes a free AI editing tool. The AI tool is a first-pass support tool for quick grammar, clarity, spelling, punctuation, and basic flow improvements. It is not a replacement for human editors, especially for high-stakes academic, research, legal, business, or professional documents.

## Services

### Proofreading

Proofreading is for completed drafts that need final review before submission or sharing.

Proofreading improves:

- Grammar
- Spelling
- Punctuation
- Typographical errors
- Consistency
- Light readability issues

Users should choose Proofreading when the document is already written and organized, but needs final cleanup. Proofreading is not intended for deep restructuring, rewriting, argument development, or major content changes.

### Editing

Editing is for documents that need stronger clarity, flow, sentence structure, tone, and readability.

Editing improves:

- Sentence clarity
- Flow between ideas
- Tone
- Word choice
- Grammar and punctuation
- Readability
- Paragraph-level polish

Users should choose Editing when the document needs more than final error correction but does not require specialized academic or business treatment.

### Academic Editing

Academic Editing is for essays, theses, dissertations, journal articles, research proposals, grant applications, academic papers, and scholarly documents.

Academic Editing improves:

- Scholarly clarity
- Academic tone
- Argument flow
- Structure
- Grammar and sentence precision
- Citation and reference consistency checks where relevant
- Submission-ready academic polish

Users should choose Academic Editing when the document must meet academic, research, or scholarly expectations. Human academic editing is recommended for high-stakes submissions.

### Business Editing

Business Editing is for proposals, reports, company profiles, presentations, business plans, client-facing documents, and professional communications.

Business Editing improves:

- Professional tone
- Clear messaging
- Reader-focused structure
- Credibility
- Sentence polish
- Flow for stakeholders, clients, or decision-makers

Users should choose Business Editing when the document needs to sound clear, confident, professional, and client-ready.

## Editing Process

The standard project flow is:

1. User signs up or signs in.
2. User submits a document from the dashboard upload flow.
3. The system uploads the file securely.
4. The system detects or parses the word count when possible.
5. User selects a service.
6. User selects a valid turnaround based on word count.
7. The system calculates an estimated total.
8. User submits the project.
9. Project may be unpaid or paid depending on whether payment is completed.
10. Payment must be confirmed before editorial work begins.
11. Admin can view submitted projects and payment status.
12. Admin can update project status.
13. User can track project status in the dashboard.
14. Completed files are returned when ready.

The dashboard and admin system track project status, payment status, uploaded files, and completed delivery files.

## Payment Status Rules

Payment statuses may appear as unpaid, pending payment, paid, or failed payment depending on the flow and dashboard display.

### Unpaid

Unpaid means the project has been submitted but payment has not been completed. Editorial work should not begin until payment is confirmed.

### Pending Payment

Pending payment means payment has been started or is awaiting confirmation. The user may need to complete checkout or wait briefly for provider confirmation.

### Paid

Paid means payment has been confirmed. Editorial work can proceed according to the selected service and turnaround.

### Failed Payment

Failed payment means the payment did not complete successfully. The user should try payment again from Active Projects or contact payments@business.editandproofread.com if the issue continues.

## Project Status Rules

## Common Customer Questions and Answers

### How do I submit a document?

Sign in or create an account, go to the dashboard, open Submit Document, upload your file, choose your service and turnaround, review the estimate, and proceed to checkout if eligible.

### Can I pay later?

Yes, if a project is submitted without completed payment, it can appear in Active Projects. Users can complete payment later from Active Projects when payment is still required.

### When does editing begin?

Editorial work should begin after payment is confirmed and the project is ready for review or assignment.

### What if my payment fails?

Try the payment again from Active Projects. If the issue continues, contact payments@business.editandproofread.com or use dashboard support.

### How is word count calculated?

The system reads the uploaded document and calculates the word count where possible. The detected word count is used for pricing and project details.

### Can I adjust my word count?

Current public FAQ indicates the uploaded file controls the word count and cannot be manually changed. If the count looks wrong, contact support before payment. Manual adjustment policy for special cases is To be confirmed.

### What files can I upload?

For dashboard automatic checkout, upload .docx, .doc, or .txt documents. For contact/support attachments, supported file categories include document, PDF, text, spreadsheet, presentation, image, or ZIP files up to 25MB.

### What is the difference between proofreading and editing?

Proofreading is final-stage correction for grammar, spelling, punctuation, typographical errors, and consistency. Editing is deeper and improves clarity, sentence structure, flow, tone, and readability.

### What is academic editing?

Academic Editing is for scholarly documents such as essays, research papers, proposals, theses, dissertations, and journal articles. It improves scholarly clarity, structure, academic tone, argument flow, grammar, and citation/reference consistency where relevant.

### Do you offer formatting?

Yes. Formatting support includes style compliance, references, headings, spacing, layout, thesis/dissertation formatting, journal submission formatting, and custom formatting.

### What happens above 50,000 words?

Documents above 50,000 words require custom review. Direct checkout may be blocked. Contact support so the team can confirm the best timeline and final quote.

### Can I contact support?

Yes. Use the Contact page, dashboard support area, email support@business.editandproofread.com, or call US (+1) 408-872-8603.

### Is my document secure?

Documents are handled privately and used only for the service requested. Admin access is protected. Exact retention and deletion policy is To be confirmed.

### How do I track my project?

Sign in to the dashboard and open Active Projects or Completed Files. Active Projects shows submitted work and current status. Completed Files shows finished work when available.

### What does pending mean?

Pending means the project is submitted and waiting for payment confirmation, assignment, review, or another admin action.

### What does in progress mean?

In Progress means the project has moved into active editorial handling or review.

### What does completed mean?

Completed means the editorial work has been finished. Delivery files may be available in the dashboard or sent by email.

### Can I request American or British English?

Yes. English preference options include British English, American English, Canadian English, Australian English, and No preference.

### Do you support APA, MLA, or Chicago formatting?

Yes. Formatting options include APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard, IEEE, Vancouver, Turabian, OSCOLA, AMA, Bluebook, business report format, thesis/dissertation format, journal submission format, custom formatting, and more.

### Can AI edit my document?

The free AI editor can provide quick first-pass improvements for grammar, spelling, punctuation, clarity, and basic flow. It is capped and intended for short text.

### Is AI editing the same as human editing?

No. AI editing is a quick first pass. Human editors provide deeper judgment, subject awareness, formatting awareness, structure review, tone refinement, and reader-focused editorial decisions.
