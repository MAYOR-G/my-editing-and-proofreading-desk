# Digital PR Plan

Date: 2026-08-10

## 1. AI vs Human Proofreading Study

- Story angle: What AI proofreading catches quickly and what human proofreaders still catch in high-stakes academic and professional documents.
- Required real data: anonymized sample documents, AI tool outputs, human proofreader outputs, reviewer notes, and permission to analyze.
- Methodology requirements: fixed sample set, fixed AI prompts/settings, error taxonomy, independent review, scoring for caught/missed/introduced errors, confidentiality review.
- Host page: `/blog/ai-proofreading-vs-human-proofreading`.
- Likely audience: students, researchers, editors, writing centers, business writers, AI-writing-tool commentators.
- Evidence needed: anonymized documents, documented scoring rules, reviewer qualifications, and raw aggregate counts.

## 2. 2026 Proofreading Pricing Benchmark

- Story angle: How proofreading pricing differs by word, page, project, turnaround, document condition, and service scope.
- Required real data: published provider prices, the site's current pricing configuration, manual categorization of pricing models.
- Methodology requirements: timestamped collection, provider inclusion rules, currency normalization, clear exclusions.
- Host page: `/blog/how-much-does-proofreading-cost`.
- Likely audience: students, freelancers, small businesses, editors, author communities.
- Evidence needed: dataset, collection date, source URLs, pricing assumptions, and calculator screenshots.

## 3. Dissertation Proofreading Time/Planning Resource

- Story angle: Why dissertation proofreading timelines depend on word count, condition, references, tables, figures, formatting, tracked changes, and author-review buffers.
- Required real data: anonymized project scopes or approved business turnaround rules.
- Methodology requirements: no guaranteed timeline claims unless supported; separate planning guidance from operational guarantees.
- Host page: `/blog/how-long-does-dissertation-proofreading-take`.
- Likely audience: graduate students, university support teams, dissertation coaches.
- Evidence needed: approved turnaround policy, anonymized examples, and reviewer workflow notes.

## 4. Academic Submission Checklist Pack

- Story angle: A practical final-submission checklist pack for thesis, dissertation, research paper, and formatting checks.
- Required real data: current checklist pages and any approved downloadable versions.
- Methodology requirements: separate informational checklists from commercial service claims; cite institutional style rules only where verified.
- Host pages: `/blog/thesis-proofreading-checklist`, `/blog/dissertation-proofreading-checklist`, `/blog/research-paper-editing-checklist-before-submission`, `/blog/thesis-tables-figures-references-checklist`.
- Likely audience: graduate students, academic skills teams, writing centers, research offices.
- Evidence needed: downloadable files, update dates, and source/ownership notes.
- Local assets created:
  - `/downloads/phd-thesis-submission-checklist.txt`
  - `/downloads/masters-dissertation-submission-checklist.txt`
  - `/downloads/research-paper-submission-checklist.txt`

## 5. Common Thesis Formatting Errors Study

- Story angle: The formatting issues that most often survive until final thesis submission.
- Required real data: anonymized formatting issues from real projects or manually reviewed sample documents.
- Methodology requirements: issue taxonomy, anonymization, no institution-specific claims without evidence.
- Host page: `/blog/thesis-tables-figures-references-checklist` or `/document-formatting`.
- Likely audience: graduate schools, thesis writers, academic editors, Word-formatting communities.
- Evidence needed: issue counts, anonymized examples, review method, and screenshots where permitted.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent data, testimonials, affiliations, editor credentials, outcomes, or acceptance claims.
- Do not use spam comments, PBNs, paid links, or excessive reciprocal linking.
- Treat outreach as manual relationship building around useful resources.
